### Added
- **Car sounds.** Engine start and stop (played when someone gets in or out), idle, mid and high engine loops cross-faded and pitched by speed and throttle, impact sounds chosen by how much speed a crash sheds, rolling tire noise (rough or smooth by surface, a snow loop on snow and ice) and light and hard skid loops on slip and hard braking. All sounds are positional, so other players hear the car. Client config `soundVolume` (0 to 1) scales the looping sounds. The sounds were provided by the repository owner.
