### Added
- **Bus.** A ninth vehicle: the roster's longest (3.7 m body half-length), heaviest (4200 kg) and slowest-turning vehicle, built entirely from spec tuning - its 4.6 m wheelbase (the roster's longest, above the indy car's previous-longest 2.7 m) widens its turning circle on its own, with the shared mechanical steer limit and every other vehicle's wheelbase untouched. Seats 8 (driver + 7 passengers) using the multi-seat base already merged to main: the driver stays in seat 0 and keeps control, and passengers board into the first free seat. A middling 20 m/s top speed, under the car's and every other vehicle's own power-to-weight ratio - a heavy, unhurried vehicle that still has enough drive to get its own mass moving. Carries no drift tuning and no downforce, so cornering still grips through the plain per-wheel physics. Registered and obtainable the same way the other vehicles are (own entity, spawn item in the Tools & Utilities tab). Existing car, truck, trophy truck, drift car, muscle car, rock crawler, monster truck and indy car are unchanged.
