### Added
- Dynamic Towns now routes the four structural wall/floor/roof materials (stone bricks, oak planks, bricks, cobblestone) placed during house construction through the same acknowledged reservation/checkpoint/settlement ledger already used elsewhere, instead of placing them directly. Each structural placement is reserved and durably acknowledged before the block is set, and checkpointed and settled afterward. If a building site is abandoned, its claim changes, or a placement is left unresolved mid-construction, the production driver now revisits that site's pending ledger entries and settles or refunds them, but only when the recorded world state can still be independently verified; an unloaded, conflicting, or otherwise unresolved state is left safely fenced rather than guessed at. Focused test coverage simulates specific interruption and reload boundaries; this is not a proven general process-crash guarantee. This is a bounded first step: beds, doors, workstations, the closing cell that hands a finished house off to its owner, and terrain/road work are not covered by this change.
