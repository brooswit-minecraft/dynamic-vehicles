### Changed
- **Truck visual refresh.** The 4-seat truck's block-built model is now a recognizable pickup: a
  full-length frame rail, a lower hood up front, a glass-windowed cab sized to fit all four seats, an
  open cargo bed with side walls and a tailgate behind the cab, fender flares at each wheel, and a
  2-tone orange/white paint scheme. Purely visual -- seat layout, physics box, wheel mounts and tuning
  are unchanged.
- **Truck item icon.** The truck's inventory icon is now its own 16x16 texture instead of reusing the
  chest minecart's.
