### Added
- Dynamic Towns now also routes five single-block workstation types (cartography table, fletching table, stonecutter, loom, smithing table) placed during commercial/industrial house construction through the same acknowledged reservation/checkpoint/settlement ledger already used for structural wall/floor/roof materials, instead of placing them directly. These five are placed only after every structural cell at the site is already placed and paid for. The remaining eight workstation types (those backed by a block entity, or whose own block can change state or identity on its own) are not covered by this change and remain on the existing path, as do beds, doors, the closing cell that hands a finished house off to its owner, and terrain/road work. Whether a resulting workstation's job-site record is already claimed by a resident or still unclaimed does not affect whether its placement is recorded as complete. As with the earlier structural-material change, this simulates specific interruption and reload boundaries in focused tests rather than proving a general process-crash guarantee. End-to-end full-building completion for these five workstation types is now exercised and independently verified by focused tests; this remains source under review, not a claim of full M454 acceptance.
