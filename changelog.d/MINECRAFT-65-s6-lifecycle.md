### Added
- **Sable car lifecycle (E8 S6).** A car's full orientation is saved with it, so a car parked on a slope reloads tilted (not upright and falling) after a restart or chunk reload; its Sable body is removed when the car is removed or unloaded, so no bodies leak. A rider leaving (sneak) is vanilla dismount; the car holds still on slopes.
