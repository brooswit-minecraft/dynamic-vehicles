### Added
- **More responsive engine sound.** The engine loops now chase the throttle via a smoothed "rev" value
  instead of lagging behind road speed, step through gear-like pitch bands that climb then drop at each
  shift point, and deepen in pitch and loudness under load (throttle held without the speed to show for
