### Added
- **Muscle car.** A fifth vehicle: heavy (1700 kg) with the strongest launch acceleration of the roster so far (`forceScale` 2.1, more than double the car's and ahead of the truck's and trophy truck's own power-to-weight), on a 2.3 m wheelbase shorter than the car's 2.4 m and a 0.95 m ride height taller than the car's 0.9 m - short-coupled and tall-riding enough that a hard launch gives a visible, nearly-wheelie front-lift tendency without fully lifting off. Own black-and-stripe body, registered and obtainable the same way the other cars are (own entity, spawn item in the Tools & Utilities tab) and drives on the same per-wheel contact-point physics; carries no drift tire tuning, so it handles like a normal (if heavy and powerful) car in a straight line. Existing car, truck, trophy truck and drift car are unchanged.
