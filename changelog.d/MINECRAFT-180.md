### Added
- **Monster truck.** A seventh vehicle built for bashing: the roster's biggest wheels (0.9 m radius, 0.75 m wide), its tallest ride height (2.3 m) on its longest suspension travel (2.3 m rest length), and 2600 kg, the roster's heaviest, with a `forceScale` of 2.4, the roster's strongest. Unlike every other vehicle, its suspension is deliberately under-damped relative to its mass-scaled spring rate - the "bouncy" character knob - so it settles more slowly and overshoots on its way there instead of feeling critically damped like the rest of the roster. A middling 24 m/s top speed and the roster's deepest engine note. Own purple body with an open roll cage, registered and obtainable the same way the other cars are (own entity, spawn item in the Tools & Utilities tab) and drives on the same per-wheel contact-point physics; carries no drift tire tuning, so it grips rather than slides. Driving over short obstacles already falls out of its huge ride height and wheel radius, the same mechanism the truck and rock crawler already rely on; a damage-dealing crush-on-contact mechanic against other entities is not part of this vertical slice (see the MINECRAFT-156 comment on this PR). Existing car, truck, trophy truck, drift car, muscle car and rock crawler are unchanged.
